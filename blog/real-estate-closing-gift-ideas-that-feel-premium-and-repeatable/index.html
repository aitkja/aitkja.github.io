What Makes a Closing Gift Easy to Repeat

  • It is useful or worth displaying, such as a kitchen board, a desk sign, or a small leather piece.
  • Personalization stays light: one layout, such as a brokerage logo, with a client name or short message that can change.
  • The branding matches the office, so the same mark can sit on a gift and on a desk or open-house piece.
  • The first approved material and artwork are what the next order copies.

One-off pieces are still welcome. A design you reorder through the year is the stronger fit, because the layout and material stay locked in.
