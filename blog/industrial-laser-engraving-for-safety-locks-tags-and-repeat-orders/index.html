Numbered Lockout Sets & Laser Engraved Padlocks

HSE and lockout coordinators rarely need a single decorative mark. They need a matching set: consecutive numbers, shared placement, and readable text on small curved lock bodies. Why Local Turnaround & Reorders Help

When businesses need practical engraved items, they often care about reliability, clarity, and turnaround more than flashy marketing. After a first lockout batch is approved, reorders reuse placement and numbering so replacements match what crews already carry. What Buyers Usually Need to Send

  • Full lock or tag count (not a sample of one)
  • Numbering or naming rules (start/end, padding, prefixes)
  • Photos of the lock body when the shape is unusual
  • Whether the job is a first run or a reorder
  • Any companion tag list if locks and tags share IDs
