What Are Lamacoid Labels?

Lamacoid labels (also spelled lamicoid) are multi-layer plastic identification plates. Laser engraving removes the top colour layer so contrasting lettering shows through. The result is a durable legend plate or panel tag that stays readable on doors, operator stations, and equipment after install. Buyers searching “what are lamacoid labels” or “what are lamacoid tags” are usually deciding whether engraved plastic panel stock fits their control cabinet or electrical schedule.

Common Uses for Lamacoid Labels and Engraved Tags

  • Control panel labels, switch identifiers, and custom legend plates
  • Electrical panel tags and breaker identification
  • 22 mm / 30 mm push-button and selector legends
  • Valve tags, safety identifiers, and lock numbering companions
  • Machine labels, asset-style markers, and equipment panel markers
  • Short-run replacement labels and repeat-order batches

Legend Plates vs Broader Industrial Tags

Legend plates and control-panel labeling focus on operator-facing text: START/STOP, mode selectors, lamp functions, and matching text across a panel schedule. Valve tags and process identifiers use the same stock class when a plant needs engraved plastic markers that survive handling. Machine ID plates and OEM data plates are a different job shape—those buyers should use phenolic / industrial nameplates rather than treating every “phenolic” search as a new panel-label URL.

Phenolic Labels as a Trade Synonym

In electrical and panel trade language, “phenolic labels” or “phenolic labels for electrical panels” often means the same engraved multi-layer plastic tags people call lamacoid.
