Why Batch Lock Engraving Matters

One decorative piece is easy. A set that must match in the field is the real job. We focus on consistent placement and readable marks so crews can trust what they grab during lockout. Quotes depend on quantity, material, and layout—not a published price list.

How a Typical Lock Batch Works

  1. You send piece count, numbering or naming rules, and photos if the lock shape is unusual.
  2. We confirm engraving fit and any layout limits for small curved surfaces.
  3. We run the batch so every lock matches the approved pattern.
  4. Later reorders reuse the same setup.
