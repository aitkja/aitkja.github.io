Numbered Lockout Sets & Matching Padlocks

One decorative mark is easy. A lockout set that must match in the field is the real job. We focus on consistent placement and readable marks on anodized and coated lock bodies so crews can trust what they grab during lockout. Typical patterns include consecutive numbers, name + number, department codes, and station prefixes that stay identical across the batch.
