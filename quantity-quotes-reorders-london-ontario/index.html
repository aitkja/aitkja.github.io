What to Send (Checklist)

  • Material — substrate and colour if known (e.g. anodized aluminum, acrylic, lamacoid/lamicoid, coated metal, phenolic stock). Photos help when the part is already in hand.
  • Piece count — the full run, not a sample of one. Partial counts under-price setup and marking time.
  • Repeat / reorder intent — first run, planned short production, or a reorder of an approved job.
  • Serialization or numbering — start/end numbers, zero-padding, prefixes/suffixes, or blank serial windows.
  • Artwork or schedule — vector/PDF when available, or a clear label/tag list, plus any placement notes.

How a Typical Quantity Quote Moves

  1. You submit quantity, material, and files (or a text schedule) through the quote form.
  2. We confirm fit on the bed, layout limits, and any serialization rules.
  3. We price from material, piece count, and complexity—then run the batch so every piece matches.
  4. Approved setups stay available for reorders so replacements match installed parts.

FAQ: Quantity Quotes, Reorders & Serialization

How do quantity quotes work for laser engraving in London, Ontario?

Open the quote form on the home page and set Quantity to your full piece count. Include the material (or best description), artwork or text list, and whether the job may reorder. Quotes are based on material, piece count, layout complexity, and setup—not a published catalogue price list. This support page does not invent search volumes or ranking claims.

What should I send for a batch or quantity laser engraving quote?

Send three core items: material (or substrate and colour if known), piece count for the full run, and whether the job is a first run, a planned repeat, or needs serialization/numbering. Attach artwork, a label schedule, photos of hardware, or numbering rules when they apply. Clear inputs reduce back-and-forth and keep multi-piece marking consistent.

How do reorder jobs work once a batch is approved?

After a first run is approved, reorders reuse the locked artwork, placement, numbering rules, and material choice so replacement pieces match what is already in the field. Say it is a reorder in the quote form, note any changed quantity, and flag whether serialization should continue from the last number used.

Do you handle serialized or numbered batch marking?

Yes. Common jobs include consecutive asset or equipment tags, numbered safety lock sets, and plates with serial fields. Send the start number, end number or total count, zero-padding rules, and any prefix/suffix text. For industrial serialization and production marking, use industrial laser engraving; for wider commercial batch engraving, use laser engraving London Ontario.
