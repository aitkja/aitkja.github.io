Lamacoid & Panel Label FAQ

What are lamacoid labels?

Lamacoid labels (also spelled lamicoid) are engraved multi-layer plastic identification plates used on control panels, electrical equipment, and industrial hardware. Laser engraving removes the top colour layer to expose contrasting lettering so legend plates, switch labels, and panel tags stay readable after install.

Are phenolic labels the same as lamacoid labels?

For electrical panels and legend plates, yes—buyers often use “phenolic labels” as a synonym for lamacoid/lamicoid panel tags. Map those searches here. For machine ID / data plates, see phenolic nameplates instead of creating a second panel-label money page.

What are legend plates and control panel labels used for?

They identify push buttons, selectors, lamps, and related panel functions so operators can read the panel at a glance. Common requests include custom legend plates, panel legend plates, control panel legend plates, and control panel label engraving for 22 mm / 30 mm devices and matching replacement sets.

Can you engrave valve tags and panel replacement sets?

Yes. Valve tags, pipe-area identifiers, breaker tags, and short-run panel replacements are standard lamacoid work. Include the full list, quantity, and reorder intent on the quote form.
