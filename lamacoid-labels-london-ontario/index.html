Engraved Electrical Panel & Breaker Labels

Printed stickers peel and fade inside a busy electrical room. Custom engraved electrical panel labels hold up better: the lettering is cut through the colour layer, so it stays readable after the panel is closed up. We engrave panel identification plates, breaker and circuit labels, disconnect and feeder labels, and transformer or MCC section tags, keyed to the panel schedule you send.

  • Panelboard and distribution identification plates (for example “PANEL LP-2A, 120/208 V”)
  • Breaker, circuit and disconnect labels engraved as a matching set
  • Equipment and feeder labels that match your drawings and schedules
  • Replacement labels when circuits are added, removed or changed

Ontario panel directories: the 2024 Ontario Electrical Safety Code, in force since May 1, 2025, added a requirement under Rule 2-100 that panel markings be updated when feeders or branch circuits are added, removed or modified at an existing panelboard. That makes it worth planning for replacement labels whenever a panel changes. This is general information, not legal or code advice: your electrical contractor, the Electrical Safety Authority and your project specification decide what a label must say. We engrave the wording you supply.

22 mm & 30 mm Push-Button Legend Plates

Custom legend plates are a natural fit for engraved lamacoid. Panel builders and electricians need consistent switch text and sizes that fit the operator devices on the door. We engrave legend plates for 22 mm and 30 mm push buttons, selector switches and pilot lights, plus larger panel legend plates and door-mounted function plates.

  • Push-button and selector legends: START, STOP, RESET, JOG, HAND–OFF–AUTO and custom text
  • Emergency-stop and warning legends in contrasting colours (for example red with white letters)

Tell us the device size (22 mm or 30 mm), the outside dimensions and mounting hole if your legend holder needs them, and the full text list. Small parts like these nest well on the bed, so a full set of legends for several panels can run together.

Lamacoid & Panel Label FAQ

What are lamacoid labels, tags and plates?

Lamacoid (also spelled lamicoid) is the common Canadian trade term for engraved multi-layer plastic: a thin colour cap over a contrasting core. Engraving removes the cap so the lettering shows in the core colour. The same stock is cut into labels, tags and plates for control panels, electrical equipment, valves and machinery. For a longer explainer on materials, sizes and colours, read what lamacoid labels and control panel tags are used for.

Are phenolic labels the same as lamacoid labels?

In everyday electrical-trade use, yes: “phenolic labels” and “lamacoid labels” usually mean the same kind of engraved two-colour panel tag. If you need phenolic labels for an electrical panel, legend plates or breaker tags, this page covers it. Rigid machine ID plates and equipment data plates are covered on phenolic nameplates.

Do you make engraved electrical panel and breaker labels?

Yes. We engrave panel identification plates, breaker and circuit labels, disconnect and feeder labels, and matching sets keyed to your panel schedule. Send the schedule or text list, the colour pair your spec calls for, and the quantity. We engrave the wording you supply; what a panel must say is set by the applicable code, your electrical contractor and the inspector.

What are legend plates and control panel labels used for?

Legend plates and control panel labels identify push buttons, selector switches, indicator lamps and related panel functions so operators can read the panel at a glance. Typical jobs include 22 mm and 30 mm push-button legends, START/STOP and emergency text, and matching replacement sets for panel builders and maintenance teams.

Can you engrave valve tags?

Yes. We engrave plastic (lamacoid) valve tags with valve numbers, service names or line IDs, and we can black-mark metal tags. Send the valve schedule, quantity, tag shape and hole size if you have a standard, and whether numbers must continue from an existing set.
