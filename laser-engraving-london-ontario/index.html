What “Batch Laser Engraving” Means Here

Batch work means several pieces that must match—same logo depth, same text layout, same placement. That includes serialized equipment tags, sets of branded pens or boards, matching office signs, and event place settings produced as a coordinated run. One-off custom pieces are still welcome; the priority is clear, repeatable output when the count goes up.
